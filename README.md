# Pharmacy Sales & Profitability Analysis (ONYX Dataset)

## Project Overview
This project analyzes pharmacy sales performance and profitability using the ONYX Pharmacy dataset.  
The dashboard provides insights into revenue trends, cost structure, margins, and performance differences across pharmacy types (Urban, Suburban, and Rural).

The goal is to help stakeholders understand which pharmacy segments drive revenue and profitability, and where operational improvements can be made.



## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance and profitability
- Compare revenue and margin across pharmacy types
- Identify top-performing pharmacies
- Evaluate cost structure and margin efficiency
- Support data-driven decisions for revenue growth and cost optimization



## Dataset Description
The dataset includes pharmacy-level sales and performance metrics such as:
- Total units sold
- Revenue and cost
- Profit margin (%)
- Pharmacy type (Urban, Suburban, Rural)
- Time-based sales data
- Individual pharmacy performance

*Dataset used for analytical and visualization purposes.*



## Tools Used
- **Power BI** – Data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive dashboards  
- **Excel** – Data cleaning and preparation  



## Key KPIs
- **Total Units Sold:** 446K  
- **Total Revenue:** $8.63M  
- **Total Cost:** $6.21M  
- **Overall Margin:** 28.04%  
- **Number of Pharmacies:** 120  
- **Average Revenue per Pharmacy:** $139  



## Key Insights
- Urban pharmacies generated the highest revenue and profit margins compared to suburban and rural locations.
- Suburban pharmacies showed moderate performance, with balanced revenue and margin levels.
- Rural pharmacies recorded the lowest revenue and margins, indicating potential challenges in demand, pricing, or operational efficiency.
- Revenue trends over time show steady growth with seasonal fluctuations.
- A small group of pharmacies contributed a significant portion of total revenue, highlighting concentration among top performers.



## Visualizations Included
- KPI cards summarizing overall performance
- Revenue trend analysis by year
- Revenue distribution by pharmacy type
- Margin percentage comparison by pharmacy type
- Revenue ranking by individual pharmacy
- Margin percentage by individual pharmacy



## Business Recommendations
- Invest further in high-performing urban pharmacies to maximize returns.
- Analyze operational strategies from top-performing pharmacies and replicate best practices.
- Review cost structures in rural pharmacies to improve margins.
- Use targeted pricing and marketing strategies to improve underperforming locations.



## Conclusion
This analysis provides a clear view of pharmacy performance across different locations. By leveraging data visualization and KPI tracking, decision-makers can identify growth opportunities, optimize costs, and improve overall profitability.
